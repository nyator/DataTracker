DataTracker

Keeps your mobile data bundle on track. DataTracker counts the data your iPhone uses, sets a daily budget so your bundle lasts, and warns you before you go over.

Getting started

  1. Open Settings in the app and enter your bundle's size, start date and length.
  2. Check your balance with your network (for MTN, dial *138#) and enter how much you've used so far under Correction. The app can't see data used before it was installed.
  3. Allow notifications so you get your usage alerts.
  4. Keep Background App Refresh on (iOS Settings → General → Background App Refresh).
  5. Add the widget: long-press your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and choose DataTracker.

Common questions

Why does the app show zero usage?

DataTracker measures mobile (cellular) data only. Data used over Wi-Fi isn't counted, and an iPhone without an active SIM or eSIM has no mobile data to measure.

Why is the total different from my network's?

Networks count slightly differently, and the app can miss usage from just before a restart. Re-enter your network's figure under Settings → Correction whenever you like.

Why didn't the app update while I wasn't using it?

iOS decides when apps refresh in the background. If you swipe DataTracker away in the app switcher, iOS stops refreshing it until you open it again. Keeping the widget on your Home Screen helps it stay up to date.
