DataTracker

Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026

DataTracker does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. Everything the app works with stays on your iPhone.

What the app uses, and where it stays

  • Mobile data counters. To measure how much mobile data you use, the app reads the byte counters that iOS keeps for your phone's cellular connection. It reads only totals (how many bytes were sent and received), never what the data was, which apps used it, or which websites you visited.
  • Device boot time. The app checks when your phone last restarted, because the counters reset to zero on a restart. This is used only to keep your totals correct.
  • Your settings and usage history. Your bundle size, dates, daily limit, alert levels and up to 120 days of daily usage totals are saved on your iPhone, in storage shared only between the DataTracker app and its widget.
  • Notifications. Usage alerts are local notifications created on your iPhone. They are not sent through any server.

What the app does not do

  • It has no account and no sign-in.
  • It makes no network requests of its own and sends nothing to the developer or anyone else.
  • It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and no third-party software that does.
  • It does not access your location, contacts, photos, messages or browsing history.

Deleting your data

Because all data is on your iPhone, deleting the DataTracker app removes all of it. You can also reset your bundle from the app's Settings at any time.

Children

DataTracker collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a future version of the app ever collects data, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
